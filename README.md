# Nehal Ki Teacher AI

**AI-Powered Multilingual Interactive Learning Platform for Primary School Students**

---

## 📌 Project Overview
Nehal Ki Teacher AI is an empathetic, AI-driven educational platform designed for primary school students in India (Kindergarten to Class 5), with initial launch priority focused on **Class 1**. Featuring **Suman** (AI Lead Teacher) and **Suresh** (Practice & Quiz Co-Teacher), the platform delivers understanding-first, CBSE-aligned learning across Mathematics, Environmental Studies (EVS), English, and Hindi in **English, Hindi, and Marathi**.
